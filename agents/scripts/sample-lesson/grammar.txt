Grammar: the verb "to be" in the present simple
I am (I'm) / You are (you're) / He, she, it is (he's, she's, it's) / We, they are (we're, they're)
Negative: I am not, he is not (isn't), they are not (aren't)
Questions: Are you a student? Where is she from? How old are you?
Present simple with he, she and it: add -s to the verb: she lives, he works, it goes.
