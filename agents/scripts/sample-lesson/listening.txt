Dialogue at the cafe
Waiter: Good morning! What would you like?
Amine: A coffee, please. And you, Sara?
Sara: An orange juice, please.
Amine: So, Sara, where are you from?
Sara: I'm from Oran. And you?
Amine: I'm from Constantine, but I live in Algiers now. I'm a student at the university.
Sara: Me too! I study English. How old are you?
Amine: I'm twenty. And you?
Sara: I'm nineteen.
Waiter: Here you are: one coffee and one orange juice.
